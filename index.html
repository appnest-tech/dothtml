DotHTMLFor iPhone and iPad

HTML files, fully alive.

Got an HTML file that won't work, or a page an AI made for you? DotHTML runs it like a real web page, reads it in your language, keeps it in a library, and shares it as a link.

Download on the App Store

Runs everything. JavaScript, charts, games, forms, downloads and pop-up dialogs work the way they do in Safari.
In your language. Translate any page in place with its layout kept, right to left done properly. Reader shows just the article. Ask a page questions, or get its gist.
Pages from your AI apps. Ask ChatGPT or Claude to save a report, a tracker or a game to DotHTML. It's in your library in seconds, and it updates when they change it. How to connect
Share by link. Send any page as a private link. When you change it, it changes for them too, and people without the app can open it in their browser.
A real library. Search inside every page, save web links as offline copies, organize with categories and favorites. It syncs through your iCloud.
Private by design. No ads, no tracking. Your library stays on your devices and in your own iCloud, and an account is optional.
