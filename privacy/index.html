Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

DotHTML is an app for opening and organizing HTML files on your iPhone and iPad, published by Appnest Technologies LLC.

We don't collect any data

DotHTML has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't include third-party SDKs that collect data.

Your files stay on your device

Files you open in DotHTML are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Data that web pages save while you use them (such as localStorage and cookies) is kept separately for each file and deleted when you delete that file. DotHTML never uploads your files anywhere.

Web content

HTML files you open may load images, scripts or other content from websites, or link to them. Those requests go directly from your device to those websites, which have their own privacy practices. DotHTML doesn't see or record that traffic.

Permissions

If a page asks to use your camera, microphone, photos or location, iOS asks for your permission first. That access is used only by the page you're viewing, and only while you're using it.

Children

DotHTML doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
